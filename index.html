Clear Scope

Unlicensed handyman services only.

Brown West Home Services does not advertise or perform plumbing, electrical, HVAC, gas, roofing, structural remodeling, or other work that requires a professional or contractor license.

We also do not split a larger job into smaller contracts to avoid Arkansas licensing requirements. If a project needs a licensed trade or permit, we will decline it as an unlicensed handyman job.
